On May 8,1886, Dr. John Pemberton served the world's first cocacola at Jacob's Pharmacy in Atlanta,Ga.
From that one iconic drink, we've evolved into a total beverage company.
More than 1.9 billion servings of our drinks are enjoyed in more than 200 countires each day.
And it's the 700,000 individual employed by the cocacola company and 255+ bottling partners that help to deliver refreshment across the globe.

Our purpose and passion have always been refreshment.

In 1960,we acquired Minute Maid.
That was our first step toward becoming a total beverage company
and begin there when people enjoy and share in life's best moments.
